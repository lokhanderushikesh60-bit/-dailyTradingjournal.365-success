How to use a position size calculator

Start with the amount you are willing to lose if your stop is hit. Divide that risk amount by the distance between entry and stop to estimate quantity. This keeps the position size tied to the stop rather than choosing quantity first.

Why risk/reward matters

Risk/reward is only one part of a trading system. A high reward-to-risk ratio does not automatically make a strategy profitable; win rate, execution, costs and consistency also matter. Use the number as a planning input, not as a promise of returns.

Educational use only: These calculators provide estimates and do not constitute financial advice. Trading can result in substantial losses. Verify contract size, fees, leverage and exchange-specific rules before placing an order.
