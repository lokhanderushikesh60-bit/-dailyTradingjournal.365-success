Why keep a trading journal?

A trading journal turns individual trades into a dataset you can review. Instead of judging yourself by one winning or losing trade, you can measure win rate, expectancy, profit factor, setup performance and plan adherence over a larger sample.

TradeVault is designed to keep that process simple: record the setup, entry, exit, risk, result and psychology, then review the patterns. The journal runs in your browser and lets you export your data as JSON.

Risk note: These tools are educational calculators, not financial advice. Trading involves substantial risk, and no calculator or journal can guarantee profits.
